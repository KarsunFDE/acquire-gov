# FAR Part 15 — Contracting by Negotiation

Snapshot of the public FAR Part 15 text (Subpart 15.2 — Solicitation and Receipt of Proposals and Information). Source: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/part-15 — snapshot date 2026-06-09. Header convention per ADR-0006 D1: `#` = Part, `##` = Section, `###` = subsection.

## 15.200 Scope of subpart

This subpart establishes procedures for exchanging industry information prior to proposal receipt, preparing and distributing requests for proposals (RFPs) and requests for information (RFIs), and receiving proposals and related documents.

## 15.201 Exchanges with industry before receipt of proposals

The FAR encourages exchanges of information among all interested parties from requirement identification through proposal receipt, provided such exchanges comply with procurement integrity requirements.

Exchanges aim to improve understanding of government requirements and industry capabilities, allowing potential offerors to assess their ability to meet needs while enhancing the government's procurement efficiency.

### 15.201-1 Approved techniques

Approved techniques include industry and small business conferences, public hearings, market research activities, one-on-one meetings with potential offerors, presolicitation notices, draft RFPs, RFIs, presolicitation or preproposal conferences, and site visits.

### 15.201-2 Information disclosure

When specific acquisition information is disclosed to some potential offerors, it must be made available to the public as soon as practicable to avoid creating unfair competitive advantages.

## 15.202 Advisory multi-step process

Agencies may publish presolicitation notices inviting potential offerors to submit qualifications and relevant information, allowing the government to advise on their viability as competitors.

The agency must evaluate all responses according to stated criteria and shall advise each respondent in writing whether they will be invited to participate or are unlikely to be viable competitors. Despite this advisory, all respondents may participate in the resulting acquisition.

## 15.203 Requests for proposals

Competitive RFPs must describe the government's requirement, anticipated contract terms and conditions, information required in proposals, and evaluation factors with their relative importance.

### 15.203-1 Format options

Format options include electronic commerce methods, facsimile transmissions, letter RFPs for sole source or appropriate circumstances, and oral RFPs when written solicitations would unduly delay acquisitions (emergency or perishable items).

### 15.203-2 Letter RFP minimum content

Letter RFP minimum content includes the RFP number and date; contracting officer contact information; contract type; quantity and delivery dates; certifications and representations; anticipated terms; evaluation criteria; proposal due date; and other relevant information.

## 15.204 Contract format

The uniform contract format facilitates solicitation preparation and use by all parties. It need not be used for construction/architect-engineer contracts, subsistence contracts, or those requiring special formats.

### 15.204-1 Uniform contract format

Solicitations using uniform format include Parts I, II, III, and IV. Upon award, Part IV remains in the contract file but is not physically included in the executed contract.

Part I — The Schedule — includes:

- Section A: Solicitation/contract form
- Section B: Supplies or services and prices/costs
- Section C: Description/specifications/statement of work
- Section D: Packaging and marking
- Section E: Inspection and acceptance
- Section F: Deliveries or performance
- Section G: Contract administration data
- Section H: Special contract requirements

Part II contains contract clauses. Part III lists documents and exhibits. Part IV includes representations, certifications, and proposal instructions.

### 15.204-2 Part I — The Schedule

Optional Form 308 (Solicitation and Offer—Negotiated Acquisition) or Standard Form 33 may be used. When using other forms, the first page must include issuing activity name and address, solicitation number, issuance date, closing date and time, page count, purchase authority, item description, and offeror contact requirements.

Section content requirements:

- Section B: Brief item description with numbers, nomenclature, and quantities
- Section C: Additional descriptions or specifications
- Section D: Packaging and marking requirements
- Section E: Inspection, acceptance, and quality assurance requirements
- Section F: Delivery and performance specifications
- Section G: Accounting data and contract administration information
- Section H: Special requirements not in other sections

### 15.204-3 Part II — Contract Clauses

Section I includes clauses required by law or regulation, plus additional clauses expected in resulting contracts not required elsewhere in the format. An index may be inserted if this section is particularly complex.

### 15.204-4 Part III — List of Documents, Exhibits, and Other Attachments

Section J lists the title, date, and page count for each attached document, exhibit, and attachment. Cross-references to other sections may be included as appropriate.

### 15.204-5 Part IV — Representations and Instructions

Section K includes solicitation provisions requiring offeror representations, certifications, or other information submissions.

Section L contains solicitation provisions, guidance, and instructions for proposal preparation, including specification of format, severable parts, and organizational structure (administrative, management, technical, past performance, pricing).

Section M identifies all significant evaluation factors and subfactors with relative importance. The contracting officer must insert one of the phrases from section 15.304(e) regarding factor relationships.

## 15.205 Issuing solicitations

Solicitations must be issued to potential sources according to policies in FAR 5.102, 19.202-4, and Part 6. Master solicitations used in sealed bidding may also be used for negotiated acquisitions.

## 15.206 Amending the solicitation

When the government changes requirements or terms, the contracting officer must amend the solicitation.

### 15.206-1 Distribution

Pre-receipt amendments go to all parties receiving the solicitation. Post-receipt amendments go to all non-eliminated offerors.

### 15.206-2 Substantial amendments

If an amendment is so substantial that prospective offerors could not have anticipated it, the contracting officer shall cancel the original solicitation and issue a new one.

### 15.206-3 Special circumstances

If a proposal involves departures from stated requirements, the contracting officer may amend without revealing the alternate solution or protected information to other offerors.

### 15.206-4 Oral notices

When time is critical, oral notices may be used if documented and formalized through amendment.

### 15.206-5 Amendment content

Amendment content should include issuing activity name and address, solicitation number and date, amendment number and date, page count, description of changes, contracting officer contact information, and revised closing date if applicable.

## 15.207 Handling proposals and information

Upon receipt at the solicitation-specified location, proposals must be marked with date and time of receipt and transmitted to designated officials. Proposals must be safeguarded from unauthorized disclosure throughout the source selection process.

Responses to requests for information must be adequately safeguarded from unauthorized disclosure.

### 15.207-1 Unreadable submissions

If electronic or facsimile proposals are unreadable, the contracting officer must immediately notify the offeror and permit resubmission. Resubmissions are considered received at the original submission time for timeliness purposes if the offeror meets prescribed time and format requirements.

## 15.208 Submission, modification, revision, and withdrawal of proposals

Offerors are responsible for ensuring proposals reach the designated government office by the specified time using authorized transmission methods (mail, electronic commerce, or facsimile). If no time is specified, the deadline is 4:30 p.m. local time on the due date.

### 15.208-1 Late proposal treatment

Proposals received after the specified time are late and will not be considered unless received before award, the contracting officer determines acceptance would not unduly delay the acquisition, and one of these conditions is met:

- Electronic submission received at government entry point by 5:00 p.m. one working day before the due date
- Acceptable evidence shows receipt at the designated location before the deadline
- It was the only proposal received

### 15.208-2 Exception for modifications

Late modifications making terms more favorable to the government may be accepted at any time received.

### 15.208-3 Evidence of timely receipt

Time/date stamps on proposal wrappers, receipt documentation by the installation, or government personnel testimony may establish receipt timing.

### 15.208-4 Emergency extensions

If emergencies interrupt normal processes preventing proposal receipt at the designated office by the specified time, the deadline extends to the same time on the first work day operations resume.

### 15.208-5 Withdrawal

Proposals may be withdrawn by written notice before award. Oral proposals may be withdrawn orally, with contracting officer documentation required. One withdrawn proposal copy should be retained in the contract file; extra copies may be destroyed or returned to the offeror upon request.

### 15.208-6 Late proposal notification

The contracting officer must promptly notify any offeror if its proposal was received late and inform whether it will be considered unless award is imminent.

### 15.208-7 Late proposal storage

Unopened late proposals not considered must be held until after award, then retained with unsuccessful proposals. Available documentation should include receipt date and hour, consideration determination with rationale, and envelope or receipt evidence.

## 15.209 Solicitation provisions and contract clauses

The contracting officer must insert the provision at 52.215-1 (Instructions to Offerors—Competitive Acquisition) in all competitive solicitations where award will be made without discussions. With Alternate I, it applies when discussions will occur within the competitive range. Alternate II applies when alternate proposals are acceptable.

### 15.209-1 Audit and records

The clause at 52.215-2 (Audit and Records—Negotiation) must be inserted in solicitations and contracts except those at or below the simplified acquisition threshold, utility services at established public rates, or commercial products/services exempted under 15.403-1.

### 15.209-2 ARRA funding

When using American Recovery and Reinvestment Act funds, the simplified acquisition threshold exception does not apply. Alternate I must be used. For bilateral modifications or task/delivery order contracts, the contracting officer must specify which items use ARRA funds.

### 15.209-3 Non-federal entities

For cost-reimbursement contracts with states, local governments, educational institutions, and nonprofits, use Alternate II.

### 15.209-4 Comptroller General waiver

When the agency head waives Comptroller General examination rights per 25.1001, use Alternate III.

### 15.209-5 Information or planning solicitations

When issuing solicitations for information or planning purposes only, the contracting officer must insert the provision at 52.215-3 and mark the solicitation face as being for information or planning purposes.

### 15.209-6 Facsimile proposals

If facsimile proposals are authorized per 15.203(d), insert the provision at 52.215-5 (Facsimile Proposals).

### 15.209-7 Place of performance

Insert the provision at 52.215-6 (Place of Performance) unless the government specifies the place.

### 15.209-8 Contract format

Insert the clause at 52.215-8 (Order of Precedence—Uniform Contract Format) in solicitations and contracts using the format in 15.204.

## 15.210 Forms

Prescribed forms are not required for solicitations in this part but may be used at contracting officer discretion.

### 15.210-1 Available forms

- Standard Form 33 (Solicitation, Offer and Award)
- Optional Form 308 (Solicitation and Offer—Negotiated Acquisition)
- Standard Form 30 (Amendment of Solicitation/Modification of Contract)
- Optional Form 309 (Amendment of Solicitation)
- Optional Form 17 (Offer Label)
