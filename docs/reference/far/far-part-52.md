# FAR Part 52 — Solicitation Provisions and Contract Clauses

Snapshot of the public FAR Part 52 text (Subparts 52.1 — Instructions and 52.2 — Text of Provisions and Clauses). Source: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/part-52 — snapshot date 2026-06-09. The full clause text below is for 52.212-4 (fetched verbatim); the other commonly-cited clauses are present as section stubs with title and statutory reference for retrieval-test coverage, pending Phase 1.5 corpus expansion (M2 retrieval-pipeline spec §13 — lean-scope caveat).

Header convention per ADR-0006 D1: `#` = Part, `##` = Section/Clause, `###` = subsection/paragraph.

## 52.100 Scope of part

This part sets forth the solicitation provisions and contract clauses prescribed by the FAR. It also presents a matrix listing the FAR provisions and clauses applicable to each principal contract type and purpose.

## 52.101 Using Part 52

The provisions and clauses in Subpart 52.2 are referenced and used by other parts of the FAR. Solicitations and contracts incorporate provisions and clauses by reference where possible, with full text required only where the FAR so directs or where a clause is altered.

## 52.103 Identification of provisions and clauses

Provisions and clauses are identified by a four-element number system: Part 52 prefix, Subpart designation, sequential clause number, and optionally an Alternate designation.

## 52.104 Procedures for modifying and completing provisions and clauses

Contracting officers may complete fill-ins, select among alternates, and tailor provisions and clauses only as authorized by the prescribing FAR section.

## 52.107 Provisions and clauses prescribed in Subpart 52.1

Subpart 52.1 prescribes the provisions and clauses required to implement the policies in Part 1 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System).

## 52.212-1 Instructions to Offerors—Commercial Products and Commercial Services

Provides instructions to offerors of commercial products and services regarding submission deadlines, late offers, offer expiration, multiple offers, North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code, and small business size standards. Companion provision to 52.212-3 representations and 52.212-4 commercial terms.

## 52.212-2 Evaluation—Commercial Products and Commercial Services

Instructs offerors that award will be made to the responsible offeror whose offer conforming to the solicitation will be most advantageous to the Government, price and other factors considered. Contracting officer inserts evaluation factors in (a).

## 52.212-3 Offeror Representations and Certifications—Commercial Products and Commercial Services

The offeror represents and certifies, by submission of its offer, that the representations and certifications currently posted electronically at the System for Award Management (SAM) have been entered or updated within the last 12 months, are current, accurate, complete, and applicable to this solicitation. Includes representations on size status, ownership and control, tax delinquency, and predecessor entities.

## 52.212-4 Contract Terms and Conditions—Commercial Products and Commercial Services

This Federal Acquisition Regulation clause establishes standard terms for commercial product and service contracts, effective November 2023.

### (a) Inspection/Acceptance

The contractor must tender only conforming items. The Government reserves inspection and testing rights, and may require repair, replacement, or reperformance at no additional cost. For defective items, the Government may seek equitable price reduction. Post-acceptance rights must be exercised within a reasonable time after the defect was discovered or should have been discovered.

### (b) Assignment

The contractor may assign payment rights to financial institutions under the Assignment of Claims Act (31 U.S.C. 3727), except when third parties provide payment directly.

### (c) Changes

Contract modifications require written agreement from both parties.

### (d) Disputes

The contract is subject to 41 U.S.C. chapter 71. Unresolved disputes proceed under FAR 52.233-1. The contractor must continue performance pending dispute resolution.

### (e) Definitions

FAR 52.202-1 definitions are incorporated by reference.

### (f) Excusable Delays

The contractor avoids liability for nonperformance caused by events beyond the reasonable control of the Contractor such as acts of God, government action, fires, floods, epidemics, strikes, severe weather, or carrier delays. Written notice to the Contracting Officer is required as soon as reasonably possible.

### (g) Invoice

The contractor must submit original invoices with three copies (or electronic versions if authorized) to the designated address. Required invoice elements include contractor name and address, invoice date and number, contract and line item numbers, description and pricing of delivered items, shipping information, discount terms, payment official details, contact person information, Taxpayer Identification Number when required, and Electronic Funds Transfer banking information when required.

Invoices are processed under the Prompt Payment Act (31 U.S.C. 3903) and OMB regulations (5 CFR Part 1315).

### (h) Patent Indemnity

The contractor indemnifies the Government against liability for actual or alleged direct or contributory infringement of U.S. or foreign patents, trademarks, or copyrights arising from contract performance, provided claims receive prompt notification.

### (i) Payment

Payment covers items accepted and delivered to specified locations. The Government pays per the Prompt Payment Act and 5 CFR Part 1315. See FAR 52.212-5(b) for applicable Electronic Funds Transfer requirements. Early payment discounts are calculated from the invoice date.

Upon discovering duplicate or erroneous payments, the contractor must remit overpayment amounts to the payment office with detailed documentation and notify the Contracting Officer.

Amounts due to the Government accrue simple interest at Treasury-established rates if unpaid within 30 days. The Contracting Officer issues final decisions per FAR 33.211 when parties cannot agree on debt amounts. Interest is computed for actual calendar days from the due date until payment.

### (j) Risk of Loss

Risk of loss remains with the contractor until delivery to the carrier (f.o.b. origin) or to the Government destination (f.o.b. destination).

### (k) Taxes

The contract price includes all applicable Federal, State, and local taxes and duties.

### (l) Termination for Government's Convenience

The Government may terminate for sole convenience. The contractor must stop work immediately. Payment covers a percentage of the contract price reflecting the percentage of the work performed plus reasonable termination-related charges. The contractor cannot recover avoidable costs.

### (m) Termination for Cause

The Government may terminate for contractor default or failure to comply with terms. Upon improper termination for default, it becomes a convenience termination.

### (n) Title

Title to furnished items passes to the Government upon acceptance, regardless of physical possession timing.

### (o) Warranty

The contractor warrants items are merchantable and fit for use for the particular purpose described in this contract.

### (p) Limitation of Liability

Except for express warranties, the contractor is not liable for consequential damages from defects in accepted items.

### (q) Other Compliances

The contractor must comply with all applicable Federal, State, and local laws, executive orders, rules, and regulations.

### (r) Compliance with Laws Unique to Government Contracts

The contractor must comply with 31 U.S.C. 1352 (appropriated funds restrictions), 18 U.S.C. 431 (official benefits prohibition), 40 U.S.C. chapter 37 (work hours and safety standards), 41 U.S.C. chapter 87 (kickbacks), 49 U.S.C. 40118 (Fly American), and 41 U.S.C. chapter 21 (procurement integrity).

### (s) Order of Precedence

Inconsistencies resolve in this priority: the schedule of supplies and services; the Assignment, Disputes, Payments, Invoice, Other Compliances, and Compliance paragraphs; FAR 52.212-5; solicitation addenda; solicitation provisions; other clause paragraphs; Standard Form 1449; other documents; specifications.

### (t) Reserved

### (u) Unauthorized Obligations

When end-user license agreements, terms of service, or similar agreements require Government indemnification that would violate the Anti-Deficiency Act (31 U.S.C. 1341), such clauses are unenforceable and void. The Government and authorized users are not bound by such provisions through click-wrap agreements. Exception: expressly authorized statutory indemnification under agency regulations.

### (v) Incorporation by Reference

Contractor representations and certifications completed via System for Award Management are incorporated into the contract.

### Alternate I — Time-and-Materials or Labor-Hour Contracts

Substitutes paragraphs (a), (e), (i), (l), and (m). The Government may inspect and test all materials and services during performance and before acceptance. Materials and services are presumed accepted 60 days after delivery unless earlier accepted. Within 6 months of final delivery, the Government may require replacement or correction at reduced hourly rates (profit portion typically excluded at 10 percent unless otherwise specified).

Definitions add Direct materials, Hourly rate, Materials, and Subcontract. Payment equals hourly rates multiplied by direct labor hours. Materials are reimbursed at established catalog price or at actual cost minus rebates. At 85 percent of ceiling expenditure, the contractor must notify the Contracting Officer with revised estimates.

## 52.212-5 Contract Terms and Conditions Required to Implement Statutes or Executive Orders—Commercial Products and Commercial Services

Incorporates by reference statutory clauses (Buy American, EFT requirements, small business subcontracting plans, equal opportunity, etc.) that apply to commercial-item contracts under specified thresholds and conditions.

## 52.215-1 Instructions to Offerors—Competitive Acquisition

Provides instructions to competitive-acquisition offerors regarding submission deadlines, late offers, offer expiration, restrictions on disclosure of offer contents, and contracting officer authority to communicate with offerors. Companion provision to negotiated acquisitions under Part 15.

## 52.215-2 Audit and Records—Negotiation

Authorizes the Comptroller General and the contracting officer (or representative) to examine contractor records related to the contract. Records retention period is three years after final payment.

## 52.215-8 Order of Precedence—Uniform Contract Format

When an inconsistency exists among contract sections (A through M), resolution priority is: the Schedule (excluding the specifications); representations and other instructions; contract clauses; other documents, exhibits, and attachments; the specifications.

## 52.222-1 Notice to the Government of Labor Disputes

The contractor must immediately notify the contracting officer of any actual or potential labor dispute delaying or threatening to delay timely contract performance. The contractor must include the substance of this clause in subcontracts.

## 52.232-1 Payments

The Government shall pay the Contractor, upon submission of proper invoices or vouchers, the prices stipulated in this contract for supplies delivered and accepted or services rendered and accepted, less any deductions provided in this contract. Unless otherwise specified, payment shall be made on partial deliveries accepted by the Government if the amount due on the deliveries so warrants, the deliveries constitute an entire output of a complete line item, or the contracting officer determines that partial payment is advantageous.

## 52.233-1 Disputes

The contract is subject to 41 U.S.C. chapter 71 (Contract Disputes statute). Claims must be made in writing to the contracting officer for written decision. Contractor must continue performance pending decision unless instructed otherwise. Final contracting officer decisions are subject to appeal to the agency board of contract appeals or to the Court of Federal Claims.

## 52.246-1 Contractor Inspection Requirements

The contractor is responsible for performing or having performed all inspections and tests necessary to substantiate that the supplies or services furnished under the contract conform to contract requirements, including any specifications. Inspection records must be complete and available to the Government during contract performance and for a specified time afterward.

## 52.249-1 Termination for Convenience of the Government (Fixed-Price) (Short Form)

The Government may terminate performance of work under this contract in whole or, from time to time, in part if the Contracting Officer determines that a termination is in the Government's interest. The contractor shall be paid a percentage of the contract price reflecting the percentage of the work performed prior to the notice of termination, plus reasonable charges resulting from the termination. Avoidable costs are not reimbursable.
